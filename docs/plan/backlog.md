## Rules

- **Who edits.** Only the orchestrator changes this file, through [`backlog.py`](../../.agents/skills/flo-backlog/scripts/backlog.py) (`check`, `ready`, `show`, `set`, `add`, `board --write`) or careful hand edits that keep the field format and pass `backlog.py check`. Workers propose changes in their reports. Updates reach `main` as `chore(backlog): …` commits, alone or with the next landing.
- **Status.** `queued` · `in-flight` (dispatched, under review or in correction) · `held` (waiting for a captain decision; `hold` says which) · `blocked` (evidence and proposed alternative in `notes`) · `done` · `superseded` · `cancelled`.
- **Done means landed.** A ship item is `done` only when its commit is on `main` (`landed`). A verify item is `done` when its PASS is recorded (`report`) and the reviewed candidate has landed. A gate is `done` when the captain has answered.
- **Dependencies.** `blocked-by` is satisfied by `done` or `superseded`. The one exception: `<ship>-verify` may start once `<ship>` is `in-flight` with a `candidate` (the exact SHA to review).
- **Holds.** Gates are held when they become actionable (their last dependency is done), not before. Account, credential, install, deploy, security-sensitive landing and phase-gate decisions are captain holds.
- **Privacy.** No health data, credentials, account identifiers or captain-private notes here; private context stays in `~/firstmate/data/`.
