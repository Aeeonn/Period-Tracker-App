# R1 — Flo feature inventory

**Research date:** 2026-10-02 (UTC). **Scope:** public evidence for iPhone Flo, including other-platform differences where explicit. Research only; no application changes or product/architecture decisions.

## Decision-relevant summary

- **Documented; high confidence:** Flo is a family of tracking, prediction, education, community and sharing functions—not merely a period calendar. Current public evidence covers cycle/TTC, pregnancy, perimenopause, age-tailored teen experiences, personalized content, health assessments and partner education. Separate postpartum/infant tracking modes were **not verified**.[A,F,P,LG,M]
- **Documented/Reported; medium–high confidence:** basic period logging remains free. Period/ovulation predictions and symptom logging are supported by Flo documentation and an independent, older review. Daily personalized insights and courses are corroborated as Premium, but most detailed entitlements have only Flo-published evidence and are explicitly **tier-unverified** below. Two Flo pages—or Flo's developer description hosted by Apple—are not independent sources.[F,P,I1,I2,I5]
- **Documented; high confidence:** current help says **basic Flo for Partners is free**, with additional Premium content (**both tiers unverified independently**). The App Store description places Partners in its Premium list. This is a real marketing/help contradiction, not proof that all sharing requires payment.[A,F,PA,PT]
- **Documented; high confidence:** Partners is read-only for the owner's health information, excludes personal symptom/event logs and notes, and shares cycle-phase or pregnancy information plus education. Linking requires an owner-generated code. Stop sharing revokes access but does **not** cancel either payer's subscription. Mode changes are disclosed to the partner.[PB–PF,PP]
- **Unverified:** Flo's public evidence does not establish category-by-category sharing defaults, a pause distinct from disconnect, an exact “see what partner sees” preview, or cryptographic/cache deletion on revocation. Quiz answers have their own sharing behavior. The project’s owner-controlled, default-off requirements must not be mistaken for documented Flo behavior.[PC–PF,PQ,PP]
- **Documented; high confidence:** perimenopause includes a symptom-impact score, symptom trends/reports, education and a **period-arrival window**, rather than only a point date. The July 2025 launch said “all users”; that does not establish that every component is free.[M]
- **Evidence limitations:** no installed-app walkthrough or accounts were used. Complete historical App Store version history, exact current 80+ logging options, many current paywalls and exact dates of tier migrations remain unverified. This is an inventory of **all discovered functional groups**, not a claim that inaccessible or experimental UI has been exhaustively observed.

## Evidence conventions

**Documented (D):** primary public documentation; **Reported (R):** independent review/user observation; **Inferred (I):** deduction, not directly established. Confidence H/M/L means high/medium/low confidence in the stated claim, not clinical accuracy. **U** after a tier means **unverified entitlement: fewer than two independent sources, conflicting evidence, or no explicit tier statement**. A documented feature may have a tier-U. References below include URLs; **all were accessed 2026-10-02**, unless marked inaccessible. Older independent evidence is identified rather than represented as a current walkthrough.

### Logged inputs → outputs

**D-H unless qualified; this documents Flo's claimed dataflow, not independent prediction validation.**

- **Period dates + cycle/period settings/history** → Today/calendar predictions, cycle-day/phase, delays, reminders, history/trends and clinician report. Flo recommends at least three cycles, ideally twelve months; says its last twelve cycles affect predictions, excluding cycles over 90 days or over a year old.[CL,CP,GP,AN,DR]
- **Symptoms/events** → dated logs, personalized stories, possible prediction changes, event graphs, recurring patterns, Assistant dialogues and reports. Flo says symptoms can recalculate predictions when cycles vary; it does **not** expose an exhaustive input-to-algorithm mapping.[LG,IN,HA,AN]
- **BBT, positive LH test, manually identified ovulation** → ovulation estimates/fertile-window recalculation and temperature chart. Positive LH yields next-day ovulation prediction; negative tests are not relied upon. Pregnancy mode removes BBT/LH logging. iOS distinguishes strip/digital tests and supplies tutorials.[OV,OT]
- **Contraception type/schedule and pill intake** → reminders, intake logs and changed predictions. Pill reminders can set cycle length from pack size; continuous intake can extend the current cycle rather than show delay. Hormonal-contraception reminders suppress fertile predictions; copper IUD retains a gray ovulation marker. Intake logging is 18+; emergency-contraception logging is explicitly unsupported.[BC]
- **Age, selected goal, onboarding answers** → age/life-stage content, trackers and stories. Pregnancy dating inputs → gestational age and relevant weekly content; fetus count/due date are editable. Due date and gestational age are separate settings.[ON,PG]
- **Perimenopause symptoms + cycle history** → score, symptom-impact/trend reports, individualized content and period-arrival range. Exact score questionnaire and gating need R2/cross-check.[M]
- **Share permission + mode/predictions** → partner cycle/pregnancy education and notifications; **not** the owner's raw symptoms/sex/notes. **Quiz responses** → separate partner-visible answers.[PC,PP,PQ]
- **Community interactions/interests** → personalized feed and reply notifications; **content bookmarks and journey progress** → saved content/resumable lessons.[SC,G]

Confirmed input families include flow (light/medium/heavy/clots, one choice/day during logged periods), discharge/spotting, mood, sex/sex drive, BBT/LH, pills, weight/height/BMI, water and sleep. Imported activity includes steps, distance, calories, heart rate and sleep. Historical documentation additionally names insomnia, constipation, diarrhea, alcohol and nutrition imports; **current exact category labels/severities remain unverified**.[LG,BC,OV,PP,OLD]

**Count conflict (D-H):** A/TR says 70+, F and prediction-change help say 80+, another help article says 16 trackers/50+. No complete current enumeration was obtainable. Pregnancy-test logging, exact stress/energy trackers and free-text-note UI were not directly verified, even though notes are explicitly private in sharing documentation.[A,TR,F,LG,PD]

## 2. Partners: invitation, consent and revocation

**Documented; H, except explicit gaps/inferences.**

1. **Eligibility:** both need Flo; main user opens **Partner → Link your partner**, generates a unique pairing code and sends it. Partner chooses **“I am a partner”** or follows the message, enters code; owner sees linked confirmation. Help specifies minimum 18, or 20 in South Korea. A mistakenly created primary account cannot simply enter the code. Flo describes its present scope as male–female, tracking one menstrual cycle.[PB,PE]
2. **Invitation lifecycle:** codes expire (duration unspecified), cannot be reused, and each connection needs a new code. Generating a new code loses sync to the previous partner account. Lost partner login is addressed by owner stopping sharing and issuing a new code. **I-M:** this suggests one active partner connection; no explicit maximum/verification protocol was found.[PI,PL]
3. **Partner view:** current-cycle day/phase predictions, cycle calendar according to help, daily educational insights and significant-phase notifications. In pregnancy: progress, baby milestones, typical body changes/common symptoms—not personally logged symptoms. The privacy policy forbids download/edit of shared information.[PC,PP]
4. **Calendar conflict:** one help article says partner cannot see “past or future calendar information,” another promises a view-only cycle calendar; privacy policy excludes calendar information **created before sharing** and explicitly permits current-cycle/phase predictions. **U:** exact forward/backward navigation and data granularity; do not invent a full historical shared calendar.[PC,PD,PP]
5. **Consent controls:** owner elects to link and can select “certain types” of information, described as mostly cycle logging. Public text does not enumerate toggles or default states. Symptoms/events, feelings, personal notes and Secret Chats remain private. **U:** granular category opt-in, sharing duration, preview, pause/resume, consent audit history and partner-side unpair flow.[PC,PD,PP]
6. **Mode transitions:** partner is informed whenever owner changes modes, including pregnancy → cycle. **I-H:** this can reveal a sensitive transition even without logged details; no separately controllable transition notification was established.[PC,PP]
7. **Quizzes:** participants answer separately and can skip questions. Side-by-side responses are described after both complete, but the same article warns answers can be visible even if only one participant responds; one answered question shares nothing. **U:** exact partial-completion threshold and withdrawal of submitted answers. This is not proof of a general shared notes/date/sex-entry workspace.[PC,PQ]
8. **Revocation:** **Partner → Stop sharing** removes partner access regardless of who paid; subscription auto-renewal continues until separately canceled. No documented key rotation, offline-cache removal, screenshot prevention or revocation latency. Previously seen information cannot be “unseen” (**I-H**, ordinary access limitation, not a Flo guarantee).[PF]
9. **Privacy incompatibility:** Partners cannot run with Anonymous Mode. Anonymous Mode also restricts wearable pairing, Family/web subscriptions, recovery/export and retained interaction history. “Anonymous” does not mean offline: Flo explicitly says it still stores data on servers.[PN,AM,EX]

## 3. Tier history, contradictions and remaining checks

- **2018 (D-H; historical tier-U):** Flo's support post described free tracker/predictions, essential insights/community, versus Premium video courses, extensive cycle reports and advanced Assistant. Thus “all insights were previously free” is not established.[OLD]
- **2022–23 (R-M; historical tier-U except the independently corroborated insights/courses):** MNT labeled personalized insights/courses/support network Premium; its community claim conflicts with current free-community help. Cosmo's hands-on account distinguished free tracking from Premium irregular-cycle insights. Neither proves a dated migration of a particular chart/report.[I1,I2]
- **2024 (D-H; tier-U):** sexual-wellness journey launched for adult iOS Premium users. **2025 (D-H):** perimenopause launch announced July 21 availability for all users, without a complete free/Premium split.[G,M]
- **Current (D/R-H; Partners tiers-U):** help states basic Partners free plus Premium extras; App Store puts Partners under Premium. September 2026 reviews report aggressive upgrade screens, confusion about free access and gated prediction-summary/questionnaire functions. Other reviews explicitly say subscription is optional for logging. These are observations, **not proof that period logging became paid**.[A,PT,I5]
- **Regional access (D-H; tier-U):** Flo also has Premium giveaways, trials and gift access. Its Pass it On press release explicitly warns it is outdated; region/platform/time-limited access must not be confused with the ordinary free tier.[PASS,INDEX]
- **Additional conflicts:** report coverage is “last six months” in P versus “last six cycles” in DR; pregnancy website gives both 40- and 41-week due-date language while help says 41. BBT's tier is ambiguous; fibroids appear in A/research but not P. These need targeted Stage B checking, not silent reconciliation.[P,DR,PR,PG,OT,SY]
- **Version evidence (D-H):** Apple's public lookup returned version **10.7.0**, release **2026-09-27**, minimum iOS **16.0**, download price $0 (single-source/U) and a generic technical/performance update. Full historical version notes were inaccessible; no exact paywall-change release can be assigned.[A]

**Research recommendations, not design decisions:** retain every discovered group in the later parity matrix; keep disputed entitlements explicitly unverified. Stage B should prioritize Partners' actual visibility/toggles/quiz thresholds, doctor-report horizon, BBT tier, full tracker enumeration and perimenopause entitlements. Do not infer native app features from Flo's public web calculators: its website advertises an IVF dating calculator, while help explicitly says IVF-treatment/embryo-transfer logging is absent. Separate postpartum mode, kick/contraction counters and concrete pregnancy organization-tool lists remain unverified.[IV,A,PR]
