# ADR-0013: Pooled synthetic backtest gating

- **Status:** Accepted (captain decision, 2026-10-30).
- **Context:** The `p1-backtest-target-misses` investigation found no harness bug or engine defect. Seed 42 tested one synthetic person per scenario; pooling roughly 1,000 persons met nearly every target. With two prior cycles, A2's weighted median returns the newest cycle, leaving parity with BAVG structurally just out of reach. The captain chose a pooled cohort and to gate Stable parity only from three prior cycles. No prediction-engine change is intended.
- **Decision:**
  - The default T-ENG-04 gate pools predictions from the fixed, deterministic seed set 1 through 1,000 inclusive. This is the first 1,000 positive generator-v1 seeds, chosen without inspecting pooled target outcomes; no seed is removed to improve a result. A 20-seed single-run throughput probe took 1.04 seconds, projecting about 52 seconds for 1,000 at the observed rate; the actual pooled runtime is recorded with its result. The cohort is fixed rather than selected from observed metrics.
  - Pool predictions within each scenario and prior-cycle stratum before calculating metrics. Stable parity is gated only in strata 3-5 and 6+; strata 0, 1, and 2 are reported only. Stable 6+ coverage is gated at 70% only for members with within-person SD at most 3 days; wider profiles remain reported. Outliers parity and Drift post-change MAE are gated on their pooled predictions. Skipped logs, irregular, and post-pill remain report-only.
  - `npm run backtest` runs the pooled cohort. `--seed <uint32>` preserves the deterministic single-seed report. Reports identify generator version, exact contiguous seed-set bounds/count, engine version, and parameter hash; pooled comparison requires matching seed sets and generator version.
  - Generator v1 irregular-generation failures for seeds 338, 341, 937, and 991 skip only that seed's irregular scenario. No other scenario or seed is excluded; skipped seed/scenario pairs are listed in the pooled report. Only the known non-positive generated-cycle-length failure is skippable.
- **Consequences:** Gate results estimate behavior over a broad deterministic synthetic cohort, not real-world accuracy or parity with Flo. Early-history Stable parity is visible without blocking the gate. Any remaining gated failure is reported without tuning the engine or generator and becomes a captain item. The fixed cohort and skip rule make reruns comparable; a future seed-set or target change requires a new ADR.
