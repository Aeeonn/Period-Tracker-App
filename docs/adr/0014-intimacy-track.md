# ADR-0014: Intimacy-track sequencing

- **Status:** Proposed (captain-directed docs replanning, 2026-10-30).
- **Context:** The partner's stated priority is a better sex life, including logging sex and desire, understanding her own past desire/comfort patterns, and intimacy content. The requested sequencing moves that logging and the couple/content features earlier without converting fertility into contraception or implying willingness.
- **Decision:**
  - In P1, `p1-log-forms` prioritizes F-012 sex-event/desire logging and F-105 inputs (protection used, comfort/pain and satisfaction) before the other detail forms. These are logs, not permissions, fertility signals or implicit shares.
  - Do not create a P1.5 insight-delivery wave. F-105 trends, F-106 and F-118 remain in P3, the earliest honest slot under the current A12 contract: A12 and T-ENG-05 calibration are specified for P3. A12 needs at least three completed eligible cycles with logged values for a metric; a candidate bin needs at least six logged days across three contributing cycles, and the chance check must pass. These are design thresholds, not clinically validated. Until then the result is `INSUFFICIENT`; no pattern is invented. A12 v1 does not analyze satisfaction, so satisfaction remains logged but its trend is unscheduled.
  - Keep F-032 and other cycle-intelligence work in P3. P3 reuses the A12 engine for the requested personal desire/comfort and mood/energy patterns; F-118 describes past logs only.
  - Keep F-115/F-117 in P2, first within the couple-space work after pairing and transport verification. Authenticated pairing, end-to-end encryption and consent controls are hard prerequisites; P2 is the earliest feasible slot.
  - Move F-104 original conversation prompts and F-053 original five-step sexual-wellness lessons from P5 to P2.5, after gate-phase-2. Require independent medical-content review for lessons and consent/tone review for prompts before display.
- **What moves:** P1 logging priority; F-104/F-053 content timing to P2.5. F-115/F-117 are explicitly first in P2 but keep their existing earliest feasible phase.
- **What does not move:** A12-dependent F-105 trends, F-106 and F-118 stay P3; F-105 satisfaction trends are not scheduled in A12 v1; F-032 stays P3. F-007/F-008 stay P1 under their existing uncertainty contract. No intimacy feature is moved ahead of its encryption, pairing, evidence or content-review prerequisites.
- **Constraints kept:** No "safe days"; pregnancy chance remains qualitative Lower/Medium/Higher and never zero. Consent is never inferred. Desire insights stay separate from fertility. Sharing remains per-category and off by default. Use synthetic data only. Copy is original, stereotype-free, and never presents fertility as contraception.
- **Consequences:** P1 users can log the requested events first, but the app does not claim an early pattern before A12 evidence and calibration. P2 couple features retain their cryptographic and consent dependencies. Intimacy content can arrive before P5 only after its sources and safety are independently reviewed. The higher-level `plan.md` summary and UX topic-phase label remain outside this task's owned files; align them in the P2.5 content task or a separate planning update.
